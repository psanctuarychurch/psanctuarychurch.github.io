# 🧭 Roadmap

> The full, current version is a page:
> **[psanctuarychurch.github.io/changelog-astro-public/roadmap](https://psanctuarychurch.github.io/changelog-astro-public/roadmap)**.
> What's done is in the [changelog](https://psanctuarychurch.github.io/changelog-astro-public/changelog).

psanctuarychurch is the church's professional face on GitHub. This is where it's headed, in rough
order. There are no dates: each step happens when it's ready, and lands in the changelog first.

## Next

- **Give from a crypto wallet** on [`/donate/`](https://psanctuarychurch.github.io/donate/): the
  Connect button is there now, as a preview; giving opens once the church designates a treasury
  address of its own (never a placeholder). The
  [pledge of support](https://psanctuarychurch.github.io/pledge/) already uses the same connection.
- **Thanks you can claim yourself:** a contributor could add their name to the contribution ledger by
  signing with a wallet, with no personal details involved.

## Stewardship and succession

So these pages outlive any one person (today drasticstatic, Christopher W, owns or administers the
organization and holds the tokens that publish it):

- [ ] At least two owners for the GitHub organization, from the church's own people
- [ ] Publishing through org-owned automation (a GitHub App, or an org bot account), not personal tokens
- [ ] A token register, a renewal runbook, and written hand-over steps
- [ ] Exports and backups of the repositories and anything outside GitHub, on a schedule
- [ ] Wallet connect: the church's own Reown project (a one-line `org.js` change, `give.reownProjectId`), before any treasury address is published

## Later, and still being explored

- **A welcome from the church.** The welcome guide keeps a space for a few words from the church's
  co-founders, empty on purpose until they'd like to write them.
- **A network of our own.** A small social network owned by the community, on open protocols like
  Lens and Farcaster, paired with a DAO. The experiments begin on the sibling organization,
  [psanctuary](https://psanctuary.github.io/). A direction, not a promise.
